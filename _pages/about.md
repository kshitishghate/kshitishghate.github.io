Hello there!

I am Kshitish Ghate. My research interests lie in developing AI systems that go beyond a “one-size-fits-all” approach to benefit society, with a focus on aligning AI systems with pluralistic human values and preferences.  

I am currently a first year PhD student at the <a href='https://www.cs.washington.edu/'>Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington</a>, where I am fortunate to be advised by <a href='https://faculty.washington.edu/aylin/'>Dr. Aylin Caliskan</a> and <a href='https://gufaculty360.georgetown.edu/s/contact/003UH00000ZmP3HYAV/yoshi-kohno'>Dr. Tadayoshi Kohno</a>. Previously, I graduated with a Master's in Language Technologies from the <a href='https://www.lti.cs.cmu.edu/'>Language Technologies Institute at Carnegie Mellon University</a> where I was advised by <a href='https://www.lti.cs.cmu.edu/people/faculty/diab-mona.html'>Dr. Mona Diab </a>. I am also grateful to be have been advised thus far by <a href='https://maartensap.com/'>Dr. Maarten Sap</a>, <a href='https://feifang.info/'>Dr. Fei Fang</a>, <a href='https://tessaescharlesworth.com/'>Dr. Tessa Charlesworth</a>, <a href='https://psychology.fas.harvard.edu/people/mahzarin-r-banaji'>Dr. Mahzarin Banaji</a>, and <a href='https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/goa/snehanshu-saha/'>Dr. Snehanshu Saha</a> during my undergradutate and graduate years.
